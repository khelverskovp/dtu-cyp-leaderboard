CYP Inhibition Leaderboard

DTU Deep Learning project: predicting CYP inhibition with molecular foundation models. Scores are on the held-out test split made by the starter repository. Each team's best submission is shown.

Your score on the leaderboard does not affect your grade. It's meant as a fun way to track your own progress as you complete the project and compare to other groups, so please follow the rules and have fun with it :)

Rules

Submit with the button above: pick the task, attach the test_predictions.csv from finetune.py, and a bot posts your score within a few minutes.

  • Tune on validation, not test. Choose models, hyperparameters and epochs using the validation split only. Only submit test predictions for a model you have already chosen.
  • One submission per team per task per day (Copenhagen time). Rejected submissions do not count.
  • Use the starter repo's splits (prepare_data.py, default seed). Do not train on the test molecules or look at their labels. The labels are public, so the leaderboard relies on your honesty.
  • Use one team name for all your team's submissions.
